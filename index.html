Codebook for Mac

Codebook is a simple, secure data vault for passwords, financials, and personal information. Protected by 256-bit AES encryption and 64,000 rounds of PBKDF2, it safeguards your data from unauthorized access. Optimized for macOS, including support for macOS 26 Tahoe, it syncs seamlessly with Codebook apps on iOS and Android.

Enterprise-Grade Security

256-bit AES encryption with HMAC page-protection and PBKDF2 key derivation. Hardware-accelerated cryptography ensures speed without compromising battery life or security.

Flexible Organization

Personalize categories, customize fields, and add free-form notes. No restrictive templates—store usernames, passwords, websites, SSH connections, or any data you need. 90 professional icons for quick identification.

Effortless Access

Full-text search, Favorites for instant lookup, and Secret Agent for inserting data into other apps. Generate strong passwords with device entropy, copy-paste securely, and launch websites, calls, or emails directly.

Seamless Sync and Backup

Sync over WiFi, Drive, or Dropbox across Mac, iPhone, and Android. Generate TOTP codes for two-step verification. Auto-lock timer and Touch ID keep your vault secure while staying accessible.

Configurable Privacy

Field masking hides sensitive data. Auto-lock, multiple backups, and restore options ensure control and recovery without iTunes or complications.

How It Works

1. Create Your Vault

Set a master password. Codebook generates a secure key with PBKDF2 and encrypts all data using 256-bit AES.

2. Organize and Customize

Add entries with custom fields, categories, and icons. Mark favorites and enable search for instant access.

3. Access Anywhere

Use Secret Agent to autofill, generate passwords or TOTP codes, and sync via WiFi or cloud services across devices.

4. Stay Secure

Auto-lock protects your session. Touch ID, field masking, and regular backups keep data safe and recoverable.
